    <h2>About Me</h2>

    <img src="/assets/images/mrs-portrait.jpg" alt="M R Slaughter" class="about-photo">

    <div class="about-text">
        <p>Welcome into my mind! It can be a bit intimidating, but I promise we will get along famously. I can tell that you are very eager to know who is behind the name M. R. Slaughter. I will lift the veil for you to peek, yes?</p>

        <p>I have been on this floating rock for a good three decades plus some years. Writing has been part of my life for most of that time. I&rsquo;ve kept it close to the chest, of course. A shy one, I am indeed. This outlet that I now share with you shall be our little secret. Maybe it&rsquo;ll grow into the world and cover it like darkness. For now, it is just us.</p>

        <p>I do hope you find something that sings to your soul, or maybe makes it skip a beat. I will not tell you I am a certain type of writer, since you can find a very eclectic style scattered around. Though I think it only fair to give a warning: things tend to get grim, since I would say I lean toward that aesthetic in life. You will find me more commonly in black than any other color. I do enjoy a beautiful dark plum, to be fair.</p>

        <p>Look at me, pattering on. Now that you have entered the Slaughter House, you may call me MRS.</p>

        <p>Please, please now go wander around! You may find something hidden within this site &mdash; or even yourself.</p>

        <p class="signoff">With Love,<br>M. R. Slaughter</p>
