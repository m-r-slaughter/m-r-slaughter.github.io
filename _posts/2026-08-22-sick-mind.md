title: Sick Mind
date: 2026-08-22
section: poetry
---

It’s all jumbled, chaotic in its nature
Destroys all sane thoughts till its rubble
Forcing its way along until the soul is under attack
Bringing questions of doubt and nightmares
Engulfing the very being it inhabits
Swallows it whole till nothing is left
What a terrible place to be in a sick mind
Find comfort you do not have one like mine
